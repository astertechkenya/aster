the process

From idea to finished project 

You see and approve every step before anything goes live.

  1. 01

    Talk

    A short call about your business, your customers and what the site must do. You get a fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Design

    You see the design before any code is written, and change it until it’s right.

  3. 03

    Build

    Built and tested on real phones. You follow along on a private preview link.

  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    Live on your domain, set up on Google, and kept fast and secure after launch.

questions

Before you ask.

How long does a website take?

[X–Y] weeks for most business sites. A shop or booking system takes longer; you get a date in the quote.

Can my customers pay by M-Pesa?

Yes: a prompt straight to their phone, with card payments too if you want them.

Who owns the website?

You do: the domain, the content and the code.

How soon will SEO work?

Honestly, usually a few months. A Google Business Profile can show results sooner, and you get a report every month so you can see what changed.

What happens after launch?

An optional monthly care plan covers hosting, updates, fixes and small changes.

Tell me what you’re building, or what’s not working on the site you have now. You’ll get a straight answer about cost and time, no sales script.
